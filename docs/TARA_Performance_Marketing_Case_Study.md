# TARA Performance Marketing Case Study

## Purpose

TARA is a fictional Germany-first cotton fashion brand. This scenario-based portfolio case study demonstrates how I would connect audience research, paid social, display, creative testing, measurement, and ecommerce optimisation for a Spring / Summer collection launch.

**Important:** All campaign figures are illustrative. They are not live campaign results, client results, employer results, or customer data.

## Brief

- **Market:** Germany
- **Audience:** Women aged 35–55 who value quality, comfort, clarity, and versatile styling
- **Scenario:** Six-week Spring / Summer collection launch
- **Primary conversion:** Purchase
- **Goal:** Validate efficient first-purchase acquisition while retaining TARA's premium, trust-first experience

## Strategy

The storefront research identified two core shopper groups: an established quality seeker aged 35–45 and a quality-led power shopper aged 45–60. Both need more than editorial imagery to purchase: product quality, fit, returns, delivery, and familiar payment signals must be visible.

I would use Meta and Pinterest to introduce the collection, Meta and TikTok to demonstrate styling and product proof, and dynamic Meta/display activity to reconnect product viewers and cart users. The existing newsletter offer would become a first-party retention and audience-building mechanism.

## Creative Testing

The creative plan tests four directions:

1. Capsule styling: several ways to wear one item.
2. Product proof: fabric, fit, size, delivery, and returns information.
3. Soft editorial: TARA's existing premium visual language.
4. Trust-led: certifications, delivery, returns, and payment methods.

The first Meta test compares capsule-styling against editorial creative, keeping audience, budget, landing page, and purchase optimisation constant. The decision point is reached after at least 50 purchases per variant, with CPA as the primary metric and product-page quality signals as a guardrail.

## Measurement

The measurement path is:

`Spend → Impressions → Clicks → Product views → Add to carts → Checkouts → Purchases → Revenue`

- CTR = clicks ÷ impressions
- CPC = spend ÷ clicks
- CPA = spend ÷ purchases
- ROAS = revenue ÷ spend

In a production implementation, measurement must respect consent requirements. TARA's current consent UI is a prototype and does not claim to operate live tracking tools.

## Illustrative Insight

In the scenario, capsule-styling prospecting creative returns a lower CPA than product-proof prospecting creative, while dynamic Meta retargeting delivers the strongest ROAS. The resulting next action would be to shift some prospecting budget to capsule styling, retain retargeting with frequency controls and purchaser exclusions, and test reassurance placement on the product page.

## What This Demonstrates

This case study is intended to show structured marketing judgment: defining channel roles, connecting research to creative, establishing a KPI framework, using controlled tests, and making transparent optimisation recommendations without overstating simulated results.
